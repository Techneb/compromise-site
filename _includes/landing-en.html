How it works

Three taps to a place everyone can live with.

  1. Add everyone

    Start from where you are, then add friends or invite them with a link. Two people or a whole group, each with their own starting point.

  2. Say how each one travels

    One walks, one takes the metro, one cycles. Every journey is timed the way that person actually travels.

  3. Pick from the fairest spots

    Bars, cafés and restaurants come first where everyone’s travel times are closest. Open the route or share the spot in one tap.

Fair means time

The middle of the map is rarely the fair place.

A point halfway between two addresses can mean a short walk for one of you and a long trip for the other. Compromise compares real travel times, so nobody spends the evening getting there.

  • On foot, by transit, by car or by bike, each person their own way.
  • On transit, see which lines to take.

Groups

One link, and everyone is in.

Send the group one link. Friends with the app and iCloud join the group: everyone’s start and way of travelling appear on every phone, and changes show up live. Friends without the app answer from the link in a few seconds.

  • No account to create, no sign-up for your friends.
  • Your phone tells you when a friend joins.
  • Plans change? Everyone’s spots update with them.
  • A group ends on its own after two days without a change.

Sunny terraces

A terrace still in the sun when everyone arrives.

Turn on Sunny terrace and Compromise keeps the spots whose terrace will be in the sun when everyone has arrived, and says how long it lasts. On a hot day, ask for the shade instead.

  • "In the sun until 18:40" on each spot.
  • Rooftops count too.
  • Available in more than 350 cities.

Privacy

Your plans stay yours.

No account, no advertising, no tracking. Nothing you type is sent to us. Usage counts are anonymous, and you can turn them off in Settings.
