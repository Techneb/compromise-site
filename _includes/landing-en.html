Names and places are examples.

  • In the middle… just not for everyone.

    Halfway on the map can be a short walk for one of you and a long trip for the other. Compromise counts everyone’s travel minutes instead.

  • Everyone travels their own way.

    One walks, one cycles, one takes the bus, and each trip is timed the way that person actually travels.

  • One link for the group.

    Friends send back where they’re starting from, even without the app.

  • In the sun when you get there.

    Tap the sun to keep the terraces still in the sun when everyone arrives, and see until when. In more than 250 cities.

  • 35 kinds of place.

    Bars, cafés, restaurants, cinemas, museums, parks and more, and you can see which ones are open when you arrive.
