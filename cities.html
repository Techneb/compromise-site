How to read it
Block
A square of about 200 m.
Going-out block
A block with at least 10 bars, cafés and restaurants within 400 m.
Inhabitants, Surface, Blocks
The municipality's own.
Going-out blocks
Counted in the municipality (the commune in France). Marked *: counted in a 24 km square around the city's centre instead, for a city covered only in its core or not yet covered.
Terraces (%)
The going-out blocks where outdoor seating is on record. Left blank for a city covered only in its core.
Coverage
Whole city, core (the part where people go out) or none.
Covered (km²)
The surface where the sunny filter works.
Covered (% of area)
That surface, as a share of the city's surface.
Covered (% of going-out)
The going-out blocks where the sunny filter works, as a share of the Going-out blocks column.
Waiting for
What a city not yet covered whole needs first.
