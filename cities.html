How to read it
Code
The first step of the ladder (below) the city has not done; done when every step that applies is.
Coverage
whole the whole municipality; core the part where people go out. The km² where the sunny filter works, and their share of the city's surface.
Going-out
Blocks of about 200 m with at least 10 bars, cafés and restaurants within 400 m; the share is the part where the sunny filter works.
Sun data
Where outdoor seating comes from (a city permit feed or OpenStreetMap), and whether the sunny filter is offered.
Home
Streets built, the municipal outline, and the listing sites linked for its country.
Transit
Transit lines built where it lies; N/A for a city with no network.
Check
The last check on screen at the city's seed, its date and mode, and how many districts were checked.
Data
The newest build date of its tiles; stale when a layer is older than its rebuild cycle (weekly for venues and terraces, yearly for buildings and streets).
⚑
Open issues; the detail row lists them.
