How it works

Three taps to a place everyone can live with.

  1. Add everyone

    Start from where you are, then add friends by name. Two people or a whole group, each with their own starting point.

  2. Say how each one travels

    One walks, one takes the metro, one cycles. Every journey is timed the way that person actually travels.

  3. Pick from the fairest spots

    Bars, cafés and restaurants are ranked by how close everyone's travel times are. Open the directions or share the spot in one tap.

Fair means time

The middle of the map is rarely the fair place.

A point halfway between two addresses can mean a short walk for one of you and a long trip for the other. Compromise compares real travel times, so nobody spends the evening getting there.

  • Each person's own way of travelling, timed for when you leave.
  • Choose between the fairest spot and the quickest one overall.
  • Set a limit so nobody travels longer than you want.

Sunny terraces

A terrace still in the sun when everyone arrives.

Turn on the sunny filter and Compromise keeps the spots whose terrace will be in the sun at your meeting time, taking the buildings around it into account. On a hot day, ask for the shade instead.

  • "In the sun until 18:40" on each spot, so you know how long it lasts.
  • Rooftops count too.
  • Available in a growing list of cities.

Made for real plans

Everything you need to settle on a place.

Any size of group

From two friends to the whole team, each with a colour of their own on the map and in the list.

Bars, cafés, restaurants

Pick the kind of place and a cuisine, then call it or open its website straight from the list.

Share or go

Send the spot to the group, or open the route in Apple Maps, Google Maps, Citymapper or Waze.

Plan for later

Choose when you leave, and travel times and sunshine follow that time, not just right now.

Privacy

  • Addresses you use are remembered only on your phone, and one tap clears them.
